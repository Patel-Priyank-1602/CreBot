Cricket Knowledge Base Version CKB-1.0

1. The Crystal Cricket League (CCL) is played with six teams.
2. Every CCL team plays exactly 12 league matches.
3. A win gives 3 points in CCL.
4. A tie gives 2 points to both teams.
5. A loss gives zero points.
6. The team finishing first automatically qualifies for the final.
7. Teams ranked second and third play a qualifier.
8. Every innings is limited to 18 overs.
9. Each bowler can bowl a maximum of 4 overs.
10. A batting powerplay lasts for the first 3 overs.
11. During the powerplay only two fielders are allowed outside the circle.
12. Every team has one designated Impact Substitute.
13. The Impact Substitute may bat or bowl but cannot captain the team.
14. The fastest century in CCL history was scored in 31 balls.
15. That century was made by Aryan Mehta.
16. Aryan Mehta plays for the Thunder Hawks.
17. Thunder Hawks wear navy blue and silver jerseys.
18. Thunder Hawks' home ground is Falcon Arena.
19. Falcon Arena has a boundary length of exactly 69 meters.
20. Falcon Arena has hosted three league finals.
21. River Titans won the inaugural CCL championship.
22. River Titans defeated Desert Wolves in the first final.
23. River Titans scored 171 runs in that final.
24. Desert Wolves finished with 166 runs.
25. The Player of the Match was Kunal Verma.
26. Kunal Verma scored 82 not out.
27. Kunal Verma bats left-handed.
28. Kunal Verma bowls slow left-arm orthodox.
29. Kunal Verma's jersey number is 18.
30. Desert Wolves use maroon and gold as team colors.
31. Desert Wolves play at Oasis Cricket Park.
32. Oasis Cricket Park has red clay practice wickets.
33. Storm Eagles have won the Fair Play Award four times.
34. Storm Eagles have never finished last in the league.
35. Mountain Rangers have the highest successful run chase of 188.
36. That chase happened against Coastal Sharks.
37. Coastal Sharks were bowled out for 94 in the shortest completed innings.
38. Thunder Hawks hold the record for most consecutive victories with nine.
39. River Titans have the highest average team score of 164.3.
40. The official CCL match ball is called the Nova Ball.
