# Our Mission

CreBot empowers businesses to create intelligent AI chatbots trained exclusively on their own knowledge base. Our philosophy, **Zero-Speculation**, ensures that every response is grounded in uploaded documents. If the required information is unavailable, the chatbot responds with **"I don't know"** instead of generating misleading or fabricated answers.
